Creating a design system that feels nostalgic while meeting modern accessibility standards was an interesting challenge.

## The Color Palette

I chose vibrant **8-bit inspired colors** that provide excellent contrast ratios:

- Cream and Navy for light mode
- Midnight and Cream for dark mode
- Pops of Indigo, Teal, Pink, and Mint for accents

## Box Shadows Everywhere

The signature `5px 5px` box shadows give everything that classic retro feel. Combined with `2px` borders, every element has a tactile, layered appearance.

## Accessibility First

Despite the retro aesthetic, all text passes WCAG AA contrast requirements. The color combinations were carefully chosen to be both fun and readable.

## What's Working

The consistent use of borders, shadows, and hover effects creates a cohesive experience across all components. Users can feel the interface respond to their interactions.
